Features

Everything a team of agents needs. Nothing it shouldn't have.

Agents as contacts

Each agent has its own persona, model, tools and folder. Rooms let several work one task together.

Approval cards

Risky actions pause for you. Approve, deny, or edit the plan before it runs.

Local audit log

Every turn in every thread is recorded on your machine, ready for review.

Computer use

Agents can work real desktops: a cloud box, a local VM, or — with your opt-in — this computer.

Automations

Recurring work on a schedule — digests, reconciliations, checks — with the same guardrails.

Domain plugins

Install specialists like SDTM Navigator, ADaM Navigator, TLF Navigator, aCRF Gen and CORE validation.

Any model

Claude, Bedrock, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Grok, Ollama and more — choose per agent.

Ghost mode

Keep Divyastra off your screen share in Zoom, Meet and Teams while you present.

Phone companion

Approve actions and check on your agents from your iPhone.

Platforms

Runs where you work.

Chats, agents and credentials stay on your machine. The local server listens on loopback only, and nothing is sent home.

Try Divyastra free for an hour.

Your first hour of use is free — no sign-up, no card. Bring an engine you already have (Claude, Codex, an API key, or a local model) and put a team of agents to work.

Mac with Apple siliconM1, M2, M3, M4 or newer · .dmg ↓ Mac with IntelOlder Macs · .dmg ↓

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: “Chip: Apple M…” means Apple silicon. Requires macOS 13 or later, and a free Divyastra account — you'll sign in with Google or email before the download starts. Windows and Linux builds are coming soon.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Divyastra into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Divyastra. The first time, macOS may say it can’t verify the developer — that’s expected while our signing is being finalised.
  3. Allow it once: System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → Open Anyway next to Divyastra.
