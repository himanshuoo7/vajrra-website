Client · Healthcare

A clear, findable website for a Jaipur women and child hospital.

S S Women And Child Hospital in Jhotwara, Jaipur offers maternity, gynaecology, infertility and paediatric care under one roof. We built its website and host it, so families can find the hospital, see who the doctors are and call or book in a few taps.

Visit the website → Audit your own website
The need

Patients search first, then they call.

A new family looking for a maternity or child hospital searches Google, opens a few sites on a phone and calls the one that answers their questions fastest. The hospital needed a site that is easy to read on a phone, says plainly what care is offered and by whom, and shows up when people search for it.

What we delivered

Everything a patient looks for, on one site.

Service pages

Separate pages for maternity and pregnancy care, gynaecology, infertility services and paediatrics, so each search lands on the right answer.

Doctors and facilities

Who the specialists are, what the hospital offers, and a photo gallery, so patients know what to expect before they arrive.

Quick contact

Address, opening hours, tap-to-call numbers and an appointment button on every page, built for people using a phone.

FAQs

Plain answers to the questions families ask first, in language a patient understands.

Built to be found

Page titles and descriptions, structured data for a local hospital, a sitemap, and the address and hours Google needs for a local result.

Hosted and looked after

We host the site and keep it running, so the hospital team doesn't have to manage servers.

Result

Found first by name.

Search Google for the hospital's name and its own website is the first result, with its address, phone numbers and the main pages listed beneath it. That is a name search, which is the easiest kind to win; we are not claiming to rank for broad terms like "best hospital in Jaipur".

The hospital's website is also a good test of our own product. We can run the Website Audit on it at any time to find what to fix next.
