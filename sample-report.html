The scorecard

Eight areas, each scored out of 100.

Functional QA · 100

No broken links, failed resources or script errors on the pages reviewed.

Spelling and grammar · 100

Every error the AI proofreader reports must quote text that is really on the page.

Security · 97

Certificate, headers, cookies, email spoofing protection and known-vulnerable libraries. Passive checks, not a penetration test.

Speed · 95 mobile, 96 desktop
