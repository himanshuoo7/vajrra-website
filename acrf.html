How it works

Upload. Review. Submit.

Upload the blank CRF

The PDF straight from your EDC or study build, with its study design export (CDISC ODM-XML or Medidata Rave ALS) — no reformatting.

The agent drafts annotations

Each field is mapped to its SDTM domain and variable, with the conditions that qualify it. Anything uncertain is flagged, not guessed.

Review and export

Ask for changes in plain English — change, remove, add or move any annotation — then download the aCRF, its review workbook, an FDA PDF check and the audit trail.

Built for regulated work

Fast drafts. Firm sign-off.

Follows CDISC conventions

Domain labels, variable names, “when” conditions and [NOT SUBMITTED] marks, laid out the way reviewers expect.

Flags, never guesses

Fields it can't map with confidence are marked for a person to decide, so nothing slips through quietly.

Draft for your review

The agent drafts; your reviewer checks and edits. Each annotation's source — CDISC rule, model or fuzzy match — is in the review workbook.

Every change on the record

A tamper-evident audit trail of every upload, run and edit — who, when, and the value before and after — for QA and inspections.

Your files, briefly held

Study metadata only, processed in Mumbai and deleted after 7 days. The AI provider doesn't train on it. Details

FDA-ready PDF

Checked against the FDA's PDF Specifications for eCTD on every run: embedded fonts, bookmarks by domain and visit, no annotations or JavaScript.
