How it works

Upload. Review. Submit.

Upload the blank CRF

The PDF straight from your EDC or study build — no reformatting.

The agent drafts annotations

Each field is mapped to its SDTM domain and variable, with the conditions that qualify it. Anything uncertain is flagged, not guessed.

Review and export

Accept, edit or reject each annotation, then export the annotated PDF for your submission package.

Built for regulated work

Fast drafts. Firm sign-off.

Follows CDISC conventions

Domain labels, variable names, “when” conditions and [NOT SUBMITTED] marks, laid out the way reviewers expect.

Flags, never guesses

Fields it can't map with confidence are marked for a person to decide, so nothing slips through quietly.

Human sign-off

Every annotation is reviewed and approved by your team before anything is exported.

Every decision on the record

An audit trail of what was drafted, changed and approved — and by whom — for QA and inspections.

Your data stays yours

Runs on your machine or in your own cloud, on the models your security team approves.

Part of the pipeline

Works alongside SDTM Navigator and CORE validation inside Divyastra, from CRF to submission-ready datasets.
