How we ran it

  • Inputs: the author's blank CRF and EDC export (CDISC ODM-XML), with every SDTM annotation the author had embedded in the export removed first.
  • One run on the live service, model Gemini on Google Vertex AI. The draft above is unedited.
  • Scored against the author's SDTM annotations field by field; the author's conventions are one valid choice among several, so some differences are judgement calls, not errors.
  • aCRF Gen produces a draft for qualified review. Your reviewer can change, remove, add or move any annotation in plain English, and every change is recorded in the audit trail.
