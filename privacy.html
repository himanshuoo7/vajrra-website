Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Vajrra makes aCRF Gen, an online service that drafts SDTM-annotated CRFs, and Website Audit, an online service that scores websites, and runs this website. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: hello@vajrra.ai.

The short version

  • In aCRF Gen, the CRF files you upload are processed on our servers to draft your aCRF and deleted after 7 days.
  • To sign in you need an account: we keep your name, email address and how you sign in.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other sites.

What we collect

Your account

When you create an account or sign in we store your name, email address, and the time your account was created. If you use email and password, we store only a secure one-way hash of your password, never the password itself. If you choose Continue with Google or Continue with Microsoft, that provider shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us; we don't receive your password, emails, contacts or files from them.

We also keep the records needed to keep your account secure: active sign-in sessions (with the IP address and device type they came from), short-lived one-time codes, and rate-limit counters that slow down repeated attempts.

Sign-in history

Each time you sign in we record when, how (Google or email), a short device description such as "Mac · Chrome", and your country. We use this to keep accounts secure and to support you; we don't store IP addresses in this history.

Website audits

When you run a Website Audit we store the web address you entered, when you ran it, whether it was free or paid, the finished report (scores, findings and screenshots of the pages we reviewed) and the email address we send it to. To review a site we load its public pages and send their text and screenshots to the Gemini AI model on Google Cloud. When online card payment is switched on it is handled by Stripe: we never see or store your card number, only a reference to the payment.

Emails we send you

We send sign-in, verification and password-reset codes to your email address, and, when you run a Website Audit, the finished report as a PDF. We don't send marketing email. After a Website Audit we send the report and up to two follow-up emails about that audit, two days and two weeks later. Every follow-up has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email hello@vajrra.ai to stop them.

Files you upload to aCRF Gen

aCRF Gen is an online service, so it does receive what you give it: the blank CRF, your EDC study design export, any SDTM specifications, and your chat messages. We process them only to draft your annotated CRF, show it to you and apply the changes you ask for. They are stored in Google Cloud in Mumbai, India, and deleted automatically after 7 days, together with the outputs and the job's audit trail (a record of each action on the job: who, when and what changed). aCRF Gen is designed for study metadata; please don't upload patient data.

To propose mappings, parts of your study design (form and field labels) are sent to Gemini on Google Vertex AI, through our own Google Cloud project. Under Google's terms, Google doesn't use this data to train its models; Google may cache requests in memory for up to 24 hours and may log prompts to detect abuse of its services. We don't use your files to train any model.

This website

vajrra.ai has no analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider processes standard request logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site. If you email us through the site, we receive your email address and message.

How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in, and keep your account secure.
  • To run aCRF Gen on the files you upload, and to keep its audit trail.
  • To send the codes and service messages you need.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers, only to run the service:

  • Cloudflare hosts our accounts service and database, and this website.
  • Google Cloud runs aCRF Gen and stores its job files (Mumbai, India), and provides the Gemini model on Vertex AI.
  • Resend delivers our sign-in and verification emails and your audit reports.
  • Stripe will take card payments for paid audits once online payment is switched on.
  • Google and Microsoft, only if you choose to sign in with them.

They process data on our behalf and may store it in other countries, including the United States. We don't share your information with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Files you upload to aCRF Gen, the outputs and the job's audit trail are deleted automatically 7 days after upload. Website audit reports are kept with your account until you ask us to delete them. We keep your account information, sign-in history and download records while your account exists. One-time codes expire within minutes; sign-in sessions end when you sign out or they expire. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account information within 30 days, except anything we must keep by law.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your account information, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@vajrra.ai. You can sign out at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we use the basic profile information Google shares (name, email address, profile picture) only to create and identify your account. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use it for advertising and we don't transfer it to others.

Children

Our services are not intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app or by email if the change is significant.
