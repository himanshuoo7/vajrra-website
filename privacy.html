Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Vajrra makes Divyastra, a desktop app where your AI agents work on your own computer, and runs this website. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Questions: hello@vajrra.ai.

The short version

  • Your bots, chats, files and API keys stay on your computer. We don't receive them.
  • To sign in you need a Divyastra account: we keep your name, email address and how you sign in.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other sites.

What we collect

Your Divyastra account

When you create an account or sign in we store your name, email address, and the time your account was created. If you use email and password, we store only a secure one-way hash of your password, never the password itself. If you choose Continue with Google or Continue with Microsoft, that provider shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us; we don't receive your password, emails, contacts or files from them.

We also keep the records needed to keep your account secure: active sign-in sessions (with the IP address and device type they came from), short-lived one-time codes, and rate-limit counters that slow down repeated attempts.

Sign-in history and downloads

Each time you sign in we record when, how (Google or email), whether it was on the website or in the app, a short device description such as "Mac · Chrome", and your country. When you download Divyastra from our website we record which installer and when. We use this to understand who is using Divyastra and to support you; we don't store IP addresses in this history.

Emails we send you

We send sign-in, verification and password-reset codes to your email address. We don't send marketing email unless you ask us to.

Things that stay on your computer

Divyastra is local-first. Your agents, conversations, routines, files, audit logs and the API keys you add are stored on your own computer and are not sent to us. When your agents use an AI model or a connected app you set up (for example Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Composio or Telegram), your data goes from your computer directly to that provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

This website

vajrra.ai has no analytics or advertising trackers. Our hosting provider processes standard request logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect the site. If you email us through the site, we receive your email address and message.

How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in, and keep your account secure.
  • To send the codes and service messages you need.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers, only to run the service:

  • Cloudflare hosts our accounts service and database, and this website.
  • Resend delivers our sign-in and verification emails.
  • Google and Microsoft, only if you choose to sign in with them.

They process data on our behalf and may store it in other countries, including the United States. We don't share your information with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information, sign-in history and download records while your account exists. One-time codes expire within minutes; sign-in sessions end when you sign out or they expire. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account information within 30 days, except anything we must keep by law.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete your account information, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@vajrra.ai. You can sign out of Divyastra at any time in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we use the basic profile information Google shares (name, email address, profile picture) only to create and identify your Divyastra account. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don't use it for advertising and we don't transfer it to others.

Children

Divyastra is not intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app or by email if the change is significant.
