Principles

Powerful agents. Firm guardrails.

Humans stay in command

Anything consequential — sending, paying, deleting, deploying — waits on an approval card. Approve, deny or edit in one click.

Every action on the record

Each turn is written to a local audit log: what the agent saw, decided and did. Built for teams that answer to auditors.

Private by default

Chats, agents and keys live on your machine. No telemetry, ever.

Any model

Frontier cloud models or fully local ones — switch per agent, no lock-in.

Specialists, not generalists

Domain plugins carry real expertise — standards, rules and workflows.
