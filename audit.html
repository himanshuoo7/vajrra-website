What you get

One report, six areas.

SEO

Titles, descriptions, headings, canonicals, schema, thin or duplicate content and how the page looks in a Google result.

Speed

Mobile and desktop Core Web Vitals from Google PageSpeed, with the third-party scripts slowing you down.

Security

Certificate and TLS, security headers, cookies, email spoofing protection and known-vulnerable libraries. Passive checks, not a penetration test.

Functional QA

Broken links and images, script errors, mobile overflow and forms that don't work.

Conversion

Missing calls to action, tap-to-call and booking paths, and AI suggestions for what to change.

UI/UX and accessibility

Contrast, labels and a visual review of desktop and mobile screenshots of your pages.
